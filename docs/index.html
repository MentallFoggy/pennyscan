What you're looking at

Every 15 minutes on weekdays, the scanner pulls stocks under $5 that are trending on Reddit or showing up in Yahoo's small-cap gainers, most-active and most-shorted lists. It looks closely at the 40 getting the most unusual attention.

Lean & strength

Bullish / Bearish is which way the evidence points. Strength (1–10) is how strong and clear that evidence is, boosted when volume or chatter is exploding.

Evidence used: news headlines (FDA approvals, contracts and buyouts score positive; offerings, reverse splits, bankruptcy and delisting score negative), SEC filings (S-1/S-3/424B mean the company is selling new shares = dilution), price move & volume vs normal, Reddit mention growth, StockTwits bull/bear tags, and short interest (squeeze fuel).

Confidence = real track record

The score is the scanner's opinion. The hit rate is whether that opinion has actually worked: every call is checked 1 and 5 trading days later. A hit means the stock moved at least 5% the called way. Until a strength level has about 20 graded calls, it's marked unproven.

Red flags

Dilution filings, pump patterns (huge move + hype with no news), sub-$1 prices (delisting risk), and a reminder that most penny stocks can't be shorted cheaply.

Automated signals, not financial advice. Penny stocks are extremely volatile and many lose most of their value.
